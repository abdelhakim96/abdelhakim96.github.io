About Me

I am a Member of the Technical Team at NestAI in Finland, where I do research on Physical AI for autonomous robots. I am also an affiliated researcher at Denmark's Pioneer Centre for AI (P1) in the Learning and Optimal Control in Dynamical Systems program, and a mentor at the Danish Data Science Academy (DDSA). My research interests are optimal control, model predictive control (MPC), machine learning for dynamical systems, and motion planning for aerial and underwater robots.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Aarhus University (AiR Lab), where I also obtained my Industrial Ph.D. in Robotics with EIVA A/S. In 2025, I spent three months as a visiting researcher at DFKI in Bremen and visited Paderborn University. I am also listed on Egyptians in CS Research.

For research discussions or collaborations, you can email me at abdelhakim at aucegypt.edu.
