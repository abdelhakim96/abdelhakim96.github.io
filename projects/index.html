2025–2026 · Aarhus Univ., EIVA & DFKI

REACT: Entanglement-Aware 3D Coverage Planning for Tethered ROVs

Online coverage path planner for tethered underwater inspection vehicles. Combines signed-distance mapping (nvblox) with a fast geometric tether contact model to avoid cable wrapping and snagging on subsea structures, validated in tank trials at DFKI.

2026 · Aerial Control & Flight Emulation

Ghost Tracking: Fixed-Wing Flight Emulation on a Multicopter

Exact dynamic feedback linearization (DFL) with singularity-robust inversion and a feasibility-enforcing reference governor, enabling a gimballed quadrotor to physically reproduce aggressive fixed-wing camera trajectories (loops, barrel rolls, Cuban-8).

2022–2025 · Innovation Fund Denmark & EIVA

Online Dynamic-Forgetting GP-MPC for Autonomous Underwater Vehicles

Real-time nonlinear MPC augmented with online Gaussian Process residual dynamics and information-based data forgetting, adapting within seconds to unmodeled drag, tether forces, and ocean currents on BlueROV2 hardware.

2022–2023 · Open-Source UE5 Simulator

UNav-Sim: Photorealistic Underwater Robotics Simulator

Unreal Engine 5 and AirSim-based underwater simulator featuring realistic water caustics, turbidity, 6-DOF hydrodynamic vehicle physics, ROS 1/2 integration, and synthetic dataset generation for vision-based underwater SLAM and control.

2021–2023 · Aarhus Univ. AiR Lab & Upteko

VT-NMPC: Autonomous Wind Turbine Inspection with UAVs

Perception-constrained nonlinear model predictive control framework enabling inspection drones to track wind turbine blades autonomously while keeping inspection targets centered in camera FOV under gusty wind conditions.

SeaVis: Gain-Scheduled LQR for a Seabed Mapping Towed Vehicle

Nonlinear hydrodynamic modeling and velocity-scheduled LQR control of a hydrofoil-actuated remotely operated towed vehicle (ROTV) for high-resolution seabed altitude tracking and optical mapping.
